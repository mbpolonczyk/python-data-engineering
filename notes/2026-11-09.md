- **My writing / speaking task:**

  One sentence per pillar, for a non-technical colleague:

  * **Reliability** means our shop keeps working when customers need it, and recovers quickly when something breaks.
  * **Security** means only the right people can see or change our data.
  * **Cost Optimization** means getting the features we need without paying for more than we use.
  * **Operational Excellence** means we change and run our systems in a safe, automated and repeatable way, and we notice problems before customers do.
